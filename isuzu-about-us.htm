ABOUT ISUZU

ISUZU PHILIPPINES

OUR STORY

For 70 years, Isuzu has helped shape the Philippine automotive landscape by being its responsible partner, consistently moving the nation forward with progressive technology and development. Tried, tested, and trusted commercial and industrial vehicles proudly equipped with diesel engines are the benchmark of the company’s passion for excellence.

True to its history, Isuzu Philippines Corporation is firmly anchored on customer satisfaction and is guided by its corporate slogan “Your Responsible Partner”. This is evident in the implementation of its company policies and thrusts. Having carefully built its reputation on the trust and dependence of a whole economy and country, Isuzu possesses core values unmatched by any other automotive company. Building vehicles that this nation depends on, Isuzu is a company that can truly be called “The Nation’s Workhorse”.

OUR VISION

OUR VISION

Ensure Isuzu Philippines Corporation remains as the number one truck brand in the Philippines in terms of market share domination and customer preference in all their mobility needs.

OUR MISSION

OUR RESPONSIBILITY TO EMPLOYEES

As in any Company, Isuzu’s single most valuable asset is its people. Isuzu believes in harnessing the growth potential and development possibilities of each employee. Isuzu provides opportunities for the employee to grow and mature with the Company.

OUR RESPONSIBILITY TO CUSTOMERS

Customer satisfaction ranks first in Isuzu’s hierarchy of values. Thus, Isuzu has the tradition of quality assurance in manufacturing tried, tested, and trusted vehicles. To maintain such tradition, Isuzu is challenged to develop, innovate, and provide the best sales and after-sales service to its customers.

OUR RESPONSIBILITY TO SOCIETY AND COUNTRY

Isuzu Philippines Corporation (IPC) recognizes its place in society to be that of a good corporate citizen. As such, its responsibility goes beyond being more than a source of investment, employment, and highly productive commercial vehicles. Isuzu is in constant search for avenues where it could make relevant contributions to the community including the protection of the environment.

OUR CORE VALUE

OUR CORE VALUE

IPC’s corporate philosophy is enshrined in three Core Values - Service Excellence,
Integrity, and Professionalism. These core values further strengthen IPC's
management system in achieving sustained growth and competitive advantage.

PROFESSIONALISM

IPC works in dynamism and teamwork. They instill a sense of individual and collective responsibility and accountability in all aspect of their work.

INTEGRITY

IPC adheres to a strong sense of fairness and honesty with all its dealings.

SERVICE EXCELLENCE

IPC is committed to serving its customers with the highest level of value and professionalism. Team Members are results-driven and display creativity and innovation in servicing internal and external customers and business partners.

Team Members are expected to adhere with IPC’s performance standards and align
themselves with aforementioned core competencies.
